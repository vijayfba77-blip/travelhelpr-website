Privacy Policy

Travel Helpr is designed to help travelers plan smarter, feel safer, and explore with confidence. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website, app, and related services.

Last updated: July 31, 2026

1. Overview

Travel Helpr provides AI-assisted travel planning features, including destination planning, neighborhood recommendations, attraction discovery, itinerary guidance, weather/rain checks, transit guidance, and hotel-search shortcuts. We respect your privacy and aim to collect only the information needed to provide and improve these services.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use Travel Helpr, we may collect the following types of information:

  • Account information, such as email address used for login or authentication.
  • Trip-planning information, such as destination city, travel dates, traveler profile, selected trip vibes, selected attractions, and recommended neighborhoods.
  • App usage information, such as feature interactions, hotel-search clicks, selected providers, timestamps, and source screens.
  • Technical information, such as device type, browser type, app version, approximate region, and diagnostic logs where applicable.
  • Messages or inquiries you submit through contact forms or support channels.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide travel planning, itinerary, neighborhood, attraction, safety, weather, transit, and hotel-search features.
  • Improve recommendations, app performance, usability, and reliability.
  • Understand which features and partner links are useful to travelers.
  • Respond to support requests, feedback, or business inquiries.
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot issues, and protect the service.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and operational requirements.

4. Hotel Search and Outbound Links

Travel Helpr may provide links to third-party hotel or travel websites such as online travel agencies. When you tap a hotel-search option, we may record basic outbound-click information such as provider name, destination city, neighborhood name, travel dates, source screen, and timestamp. This helps us understand user interest and improve future travel and partner experiences.

Once you leave Travel Helpr and visit a third-party website, that website’s own privacy policy and terms apply. Travel Helpr is not responsible for the content, pricing, availability, booking flow, or privacy practices of third-party websites.

5. Authentication and Third-Party Services

Travel Helpr may use third-party services for authentication, hosting, analytics, database storage, maps, weather, and other travel-planning functionality. These providers may process limited information as needed to operate the service.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website or third-party services may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to support basic functionality, improve performance, analyze usage, or remember preferences. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with trusted service providers only when needed to operate, secure, analyze, or improve Travel Helpr. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of users, the public, or Travel Helpr.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security. You should use Travel Helpr responsibly and avoid submitting sensitive personal information unless required.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, improve the product, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain operational records. We may retain aggregated or de-identified analytics data for longer periods.

10. Children’s Privacy

Travel Helpr is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

11. Your Choices

You may contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of certain information associated with your use of Travel Helpr, subject to applicable laws and operational requirements.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of Travel Helpr after updates means you accept the revised policy.
