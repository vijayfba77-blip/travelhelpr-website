About TravelHelpr

Making every journey feel simpler, safer, and more meaningful.

Why TravelHelpr Exists

TravelHelpr was born from a simple idea — that planning a trip should feel exciting, not overwhelming. Whether you’re visiting a city for the first time or returning to a familiar place, the questions remain the same: Where should I stay? Which attractions are worth exploring? Which places match my interests? What should I include in my itinerary?

To empower travelers with meaningful recommendations and practical insights that help them make smarter trip-planning decisions and explore with greater confidence. We believe travel isn’t just about reaching places — it’s about connecting, learning, and growing.

Our Mission

To empower travelers with meaningful information and real-time insights that help them make smarter decisions, stay safe, and truly enjoy every moment of their journey. We believe travel isn’t just about reaching places — it’s about connecting, learning, and growing.

Meet the Founder

🇺🇸 TravelHelpr is launching first for destinations across the United States.

Vijay Kumar – Founder, TravelHelpr

Vijay’s idea for TravelHelpr came from his own travel experiences — especially the challenges of choosing the right neighborhood, identifying worthwhile attractions, and organizing a trip efficiently while traveling with family. After years of working in technology and global delivery roles, he decided to build a product that combines practical travel needs with data-driven recommendations.
